Scope

What is included and where specialist advice is needed

Included in our practical support

  • Practical market and location research
  • Local meetings and coordination
  • Introductions to relevant third-party professionals
  • Supplier and service-provider coordination
  • Progress updates and local follow-up

Not provided by Bridge to England

  • Legal, tax or immigration advice
  • Company formation or accountancy services provided by us directly
  • Regulated financial or investment advice
  • Guarantees of commercial success or market outcomes

Where regulated, legal or specialist professional advice is required, we can help coordinate the appropriate qualified adviser, but their advice remains independent from Bridge to England.
