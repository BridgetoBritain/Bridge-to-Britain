Scope

What is included and where specialist advice is needed

Included in our practical support

  • Scope and priority coordination
  • Quotation collection and comparison support
  • Contractor communication and access coordination
  • Agreed progress visits and photo updates
  • Practical snagging observations and handover coordination

Not provided by Bridge to England

  • Architectural or structural engineering design
  • Building Control approval or statutory certification
  • Gas, electrical or other regulated trade certification by us
  • Contractor warranties or guarantees from Bridge to England
  • Formal contract administration unless separately agreed with an appropriately qualified professional

Where regulated, legal or specialist professional advice is required, we can help coordinate the appropriate qualified adviser, but their advice remains independent from Bridge to England.
