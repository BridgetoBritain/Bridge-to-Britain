How The Process Works

A straightforward four-step approach

Agree the instruction

We confirm exactly what you need us to do and the limits of the instruction.

Confirm access and contacts

We establish the relevant people, location, timing and access arrangements.

Attend or coordinate

We carry out the agreed local action and record the practical outcome.

Report and follow up

We update you clearly and follow agreed next actions where required.

Scope

What is included and where specialist advice is needed

Included in our practical support

  • Agreed meeting or site attendance
  • Local communication and coordination
  • Access, keys and appointment support
  • Practical progress follow-up
  • Written, photo or video updates where appropriate

Not provided by Bridge to England

  • Legal representation or power of attorney
  • Regulated professional advice
  • Signing contracts or committing funds unless separately and lawfully authorised
  • Guarantees of third-party performance
  • Activities outside an agreed written scope

Where regulated, legal or specialist professional advice is required, we can help coordinate the appropriate qualified adviser, but their advice remains independent from Bridge to England.
