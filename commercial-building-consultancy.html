Scope

What is included and where specialist advice is needed

Included in our practical support

  • Practical visible observations
  • M&E and maintenance context
  • PPM and contractor coordination support
  • Quotation and remedial-action follow-up
  • Practical owner or client reporting

Not provided by Bridge to England

  • Formal structural or building survey
  • Fire risk assessment or statutory compliance certification
  • Electrical inspection certification or gas work by us
  • Professional engineering design requiring protected or specialist sign-off
  • Guarantees that visible observations identify every defect

Where regulated, legal or specialist professional advice is required, we can help coordinate the appropriate qualified adviser, but their advice remains independent from Bridge to England.
