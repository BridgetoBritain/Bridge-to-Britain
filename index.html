About Bridge to Britain

Built on local knowledge, commercial experience and trust.

Bridge to Britain helps Turkish businesses, entrepreneurs and investors establish, expand and invest successfully in the United Kingdom.

Founded by Erman Bahadir, a British citizen originally from Türkiye who has lived in London since 1995, Bridge to Britain combines local market understanding with practical commercial experience.

With a background in mathematics, commercial building engineering, project coordination and property investment, Erman provides bilingual support from initial planning through to successful project delivery.
