Bridge to Britain supports Turkish businesses, entrepreneurs and investors looking to establish, expand and invest successfully in the United Kingdom.

Founded by Erman Bahadir, a British citizen originally from Türkiye who has lived in London since 1995, Bridge to Britain combines local market understanding with practical commercial experience.

With a background in mathematics, commercial building engineering, project coordination and property investment, Erman provides independent and bilingual support throughout each client’s UK journey.
