Meet Ethan

Ethan Erman Bahadir

Founder & Managing Director

Bridge to Britain was founded to help Turkish businesses, entrepreneurs and investors confidently establish, invest and grow in the United Kingdom.

A British citizen originally from Türkiye who has lived in London since 1995, Ethan combines local market understanding with practical commercial experience.

With a background in mathematics, commercial building engineering, project coordination and property investment, he provides independent and bilingual support from initial planning through to successful delivery.
