About Bridge to Britain

Local knowledge. Practical experience. Trusted support.

Bridge to Britain supports Turkish businesses, entrepreneurs and investors looking to establish, expand and invest successfully in the United Kingdom.

Founded by Erman Bahadir, a British citizen originally from Türkiye who has lived in London since 1995, the company combines local market understanding with practical commercial experience.

With a background in mathematics, commercial building engineering, project coordination and property investment, Erman provides independent and bilingual support from initial planning through to successful project delivery.
